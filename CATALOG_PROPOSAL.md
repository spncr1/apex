Names and primary group assignments are grounded in the [ACE Exercise Library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/). The library's group lists are exercise categories, not claims that an exercise isolates one muscle. User-owned groups and exercises remain separate from these shared rows.

## Subdivisions and anatomy fit

The [ACE taxonomy](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/) supports these optional labels: Abdominals (rectus abdominis, obliques); Calves (gastrocnemius, soleus); Forearms (flexors, extensors); Glutes (gluteus maximus, gluteus medius/minimus); Shoulders (anterior/medial and posterior deltoids). [Pectoralis major anatomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK525991/) supports clavicular and sternocostal heads. The mockup's “Middle Chest” is a training label, not a separate anatomical head; the initial catalog should omit it unless the owner expressly wants a non-anatomical filter.

The starter exercises are mostly mapped to whole groups. The seed maps Crunch to rectus abdominis and Side Plank to obliques, based on [ACE's rectus category](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/body-part/abs/rectus-abdominus%28abs%29/) and [ACE's lateral-core description](https://www.acefitness.org/cmes-resources/pdfs/02-10-CMES-McGillsTorsoEnduracneTest.pdf). The wrist curls map to flexors and extensors using [ACE's flexion description](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/30/wrist-curl-flexion/) and [extension description](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/29/wrist-curl-extension/). The [bench-angle evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34644424/) does not directly test the cable-machine variants in this catalog, so the three chest exercises remain assigned to the whole chest group. A subdivision filter with no mapped exercises correctly shows an empty list rather than claiming an unsupported association.

[Anatome](https://github.com/Rippy1911/anatome) provides whole-group SVG muscle regions. Its live `/listMuscles` and `/bodyPaths` endpoints were checked on 7 November 2026. They include `chest`, `calves`, `forearm`, `gluteal`, `hamstring`, `quadriceps`, `deltoids`, `triceps`, and `upper-back`; `abs` and `obliques` are separate. The bundled `assets/anatomy/anatome-body-paths.json` is an unchanged copy of its path response and supports static views plus the supported tappable regions. No separate paths exist for the proposed chest, calf, forearm, glute, or deltoid subdivisions. Those filters use text chips; no paths are invented from the mockup.

[Anatome's NOTICE](https://github.com/Rippy1911/anatome/blob/main/NOTICE) identifies the anatomical path data as MIT licensed from react-native-body-highlighter. The upstream MIT license is retained in `assets/anatomy/LICENSE-MIT.txt`. No exercise photos or videos are bundled. The supplied superhero images are references only until their production assets are available.
